# Glossary

Plain-language meanings of the words The Logbook uses, and of the
abbreviations you will meet in these guides. Where a word means something
specific in this app, the meaning here is the app's meaning.

**Back to:** [Training guides](./README.md) · [Getting Started](./00-getting-started.md)

---

## Words the App Uses

| Word                     | What it means                                                                                                                                                                               |
| ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Access Denied**        | The message you see when your position doesn't include the page you opened. Trying again won't help; ask an officer if you need it                                                          |
| **Acknowledge**          | A button on some department messages. Selecting it records that you have read the message                                                                                                   |
| **Administrator**        | Someone whose position lets them change department-wide settings, such as which parts of the app are turned on                                                                              |
| **Apparatus**            | A department vehicle, such as an engine, ladder truck or ambulance                                                                                                                          |
| **Approval**             | A request, such as a purchase or a training record, that waits for a named officer to accept or turn it down                                                                                |
| **Audit log**            | A permanent record of who changed what, and when. Officers use it to check what happened                                                                                                    |
| **Badge code**           | The code in the barcode on your ID card. Scanning it identifies you                                                                                                                         |
| **Catch-up deadline**    | A later date by which members who joined before a new requirement must meet it. Until then, the requirement counts neither for nor against them                                             |
| **Certification**        | A qualification with an expiry date, such as EMT or CPR                                                                                                                                     |
| **Check-in**             | Recording that you arrived at an event or shift, often by scanning a QR code or tapping your ID card                                                                                        |
| **Compliance**           | Whether members have met the department's required training, screenings and attendance. **Compliant** means done; **At Risk** means close to falling behind; **Non-Compliant** means behind |
| **Dashboard**            | Your home page in The Logbook. It opens after you sign in                                                                                                                                   |
| **Department time zone** | Times and dates in The Logbook are shown in your department's time zone, so "today" means the department's today                                                                            |
| **Dialog**               | A small window that opens over the page, usually holding a form or a question. Close it with **Escape** or the **X**                                                                        |
| **Enrollment**           | Your place in a training program, with your progress through it                                                                                                                             |
| **Finalize attendance**  | When an officer locks the attendance list for an event or shift, so the hours are credited                                                                                                  |
| **Issued gear**          | Equipment the department has given you to keep and look after, such as turnout gear. Listed under **My Issued Gear**                                                                        |
| **Leave of absence**     | Approved time away. While it lasts, requirements counted in hours, shifts or calls are reduced so you aren't marked down for time you were away                                             |
| **Member class**         | Whether you are an operational, administrative or social member                                                                                                                             |
| **Membership status**    | Where you are in your membership, for example prospective, probationary, regular, life, retired or honorary                                                                                 |
| **Module**               | One part of the app, such as Training, Scheduling or Inventory. Your department chooses which modules are turned on                                                                         |
| **Notification**         | A message from The Logbook about something that affects you, such as a new shift or an event reminder. Shown in your inbox and on your dashboard                                            |
| **Offline**              | Using The Logbook on your phone without a signal. Some things you enter are saved on the phone and sent when the signal returns                                                             |
| **Open shift**           | A shift with a free seat that eligible members can sign up for                                                                                                                              |
| **Permission**           | One thing a position allows you to do, such as "manage training" or "view reports". You get permissions from your positions, never on your own                                              |
| **Persistent message**   | A department message that stays on your dashboard after you read it, until an officer removes it                                                                                            |
| **Pin**                  | Keep an item at the top of a list, such as a notification or an inventory item                                                                                                              |
| **Pipeline**             | The steps an applicant goes through to become a member. Each step is a **stage**                                                                                                            |
| **Platoon**              | A group of members who work the same rotating schedule of shifts                                                                                                                            |
| **Position**             | A job in the department, such as Training Officer or Quartermaster. Each position comes with a set of permissions. You can hold more than one                                               |
| **Program**              | A planned course of training, such as recruit school, made up of phases and requirements                                                                                                    |
| **Prospective member**   | Someone who has applied to join but is not a member yet. Also called an applicant                                                                                                           |
| **Proxy vote**           | A vote cast for you by someone you have authorized, when you can't vote yourself                                                                                                            |
| **Quartermaster**        | The officer who looks after the department's gear and supplies                                                                                                                              |
| **Rank**                 | Your title, such as Firefighter, Lieutenant or Captain. Your rank is separate from your positions                                                                                           |
| **Recovery code**        | A one-time code you can use to sign in if you lose the phone that gives you your sign-in codes. Each one works once                                                                         |
| **Requirement**          | Something you must complete in a set period, such as a number of training hours, a course, shifts or calls                                                                                  |
| **RSVP**                 | Telling the department whether you will attend an event                                                                                                                                     |
| **Shift report**         | An officer's summary of a shift. It credits the hours and calls worked                                                                                                                      |
| **Single sign-on**       | Signing in to The Logbook with your work Google or Microsoft account instead of a Logbook password                                                                                          |
| **Swap**                 | Trading a shift. An **open swap** is offered to members qualified for your seat, and the first one to pick it up takes the seat                                                             |
| **Two-step sign-in**     | Entering a code from your phone after your password, so a stolen password alone can't open your account. Also called MFA                                                                    |
| **Waiver**               | An officer-approved exemption from a requirement for a set period                                                                                                                           |

## Abbreviations

| Abbreviation | Stands for                                             | What it means here                                                                                            |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **CPR**      | Cardiopulmonary resuscitation                          | A common certification with an expiry date                                                                    |
| **CSV**      | Comma-separated values                                 | A simple spreadsheet file. It opens in Excel or Google Sheets                                                 |
| **EMS**      | Emergency medical services                             | Ambulance and medical response                                                                                |
| **EMT**      | Emergency medical technician                           | A medical certification                                                                                       |
| **EVOC**     | Emergency Vehicle Operations Course                    | The driver training needed before you can drive apparatus                                                     |
| **HIPAA**    | Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act    | The US health-privacy law. It is why medical records in The Logbook are restricted                            |
| **ICS**      | Incident Command System (or, for calendars, iCalendar) | In training: the standard incident structure. In calendars: a file or link your calendar app can subscribe to |
| **ISO**      | International Organization for Standardization         | Publishes standards, including some the department's records follow                                           |
| **MFA**      | Multi-factor authentication                            | Two-step sign-in: your password plus a code from your phone                                                   |
| **NFC**      | Near-field communication                               | The tap technology in some ID cards and tags. You hold the card near a phone or reader instead of scanning    |
| **NFPA**     | National Fire Protection Association                   | Publishes fire-service standards, such as NFPA 1851 for the care of turnout gear                              |
| **NREMT**    | National Registry of Emergency Medical Technicians     | Certifies EMTs and paramedics; its skills sheets are used in skills testing                                   |
| **OSHA**     | Occupational Safety and Health Administration          | The US workplace safety agency                                                                                |
| **PDF**      | Portable Document Format                               | A document file that prints and looks the same everywhere                                                     |
| **PHI**      | Protected health information                           | Health details about a person that the law requires to be kept private                                        |
| **PPE**      | Personal protective equipment                          | Your protective gear: helmet, coat, pants, boots, gloves                                                      |
| **PWA**      | Progressive web app                                    | The Logbook added to your phone's home screen so it opens like an app                                         |
| **QR code**  | Quick response code                                    | A square barcode your phone's camera can scan to open a page or check in                                      |
| **SCBA**     | Self-contained breathing apparatus                     | An air pack                                                                                                   |
| **SMS**      | Short message service                                  | A text message                                                                                                |
| **SOP**      | Standard operating procedure                           | A written department procedure                                                                                |
| **SSO**      | Single sign-on                                         | Signing in with your work Google or Microsoft account                                                         |
