# Video Script — Apparatus Events: Crews Without Shifts

A narration script and shot list for a short training video (about two
minutes) on apparatus events: the Santamobile, parades, touch-a-truck and any
other outreach that puts an apparatus on the road with a crew but is not a
shift.

The reference text is
[Apparatus Events: Crews Without Shifts](../04-events-meetings.md#apparatus-events-crews-without-shifts)
in the Events guide. When the two disagree, the guide is right and this script
needs updating.

## How the video is meant to be made

The screen recording is silent and carries an on-screen caption per scene.
Record the narration below over it, then upload. The times are where each
scene starts in the silent recording made on 2026-11-17 (2:05 long, 1280×720).
A re-recording will drift by a second or two; match the narration to the
captions, not to the clock.

The recording uses demonstration data: _Santamobile — Full_ on Engine 1 with
Driver, Navigator, Santa and four Elves; a five-night run in December; a
Holiday Parade that collides with one night; and a scheduled Scout Troop 12
station tour on the Outreach Requests tab. No real member appears in it.

**Labels changed since the recording (2026-11-21).** The tab the recording
shows as **Requests** is now **Outreach Requests**, and the **Community
Outreach** card now opens on a new **Overview** page; **Apparatus Events** is
the fourth page in the strip rather than the third. Scenes 1 and 8 show the old
strip. The narration does not name either label and still holds; re-record
those two scenes' opening seconds (about 5 each) when the video is next
refreshed.

## Scenes

### 1. Where it lives — 0:00

**On screen:** Events Administration, then the Community Outreach area
(it opens on Overview), then Apparatus Events and its template cards.

**Caption:** Events Administration → Community Outreach → Apparatus Events

**Narration:**

> Some of our outreach puts an apparatus on the road with a crew — the
> Santamobile, a holiday parade, a touch-a-truck. That isn't a shift, and it
> shouldn't be recorded as one. Apparatus Events are how we plan these. You'll
> find them under Events Administration, in Community Outreach.

### 2. A template is the plan — 0:10

**On screen:** the template cards; the Edit form for _Santamobile — Full_,
scrolling through its seats, the Driver EVOC rule and the Admin Hours
category.

**Captions:** A template is the crew plan you reuse… · Seats are free text…
· Driver EVOC rule… · Choose the Admin Hours category…

**Narration:**

> A template is the plan you reuse every year. It names the apparatus and the
> seats on it. Seat names are whatever your department calls them — Driver,
> Navigator, Santa, Elf — and you can ask for several of one seat. The seat
> that actually drives is marked Driver, and the template decides how strictly
> EVOC is checked for it: Enforce, which uses the same rule shifts do; Warn
> only; or Don't check, for a static display. Last, pick the Admin Hours
> category. The crew's time goes there — never to shift hours.

### 3. Duplicate for a smaller or larger crew — 0:30

**On screen:** Duplicate on the template card; the copy renamed
_Santamobile — Small_, Navigator set to 0, Elves set to 2.

**Captions:** Need a smaller version? Duplicate the template · Set a count
for each seat. 0 leaves a seat off the copy

**Narration:**

> Short-handed weeknights? Duplicate the template, give the copy a name, and
> set how many of each seat it needs. Zero leaves a seat off. The original
> isn't touched.

### 4. Schedule one night or many — 0:43

**On screen:** Schedule nights; three December nights entered; the third
flagged because Engine 1 is already on the Holiday Parade; Schedule anyway
ticked; Create 3 nights.

**Captions:** Schedule nights turns a template into one or many events · Each
night gets its own crew… · Engine 1 is already on the Holiday Parade… · The
nights are created as ordinary events

**Narration:**

> Schedule nights turns the template into events — one night or a whole run.
> Each night is its own event with its own crew, so members sign up for the
> nights they can make. If the apparatus is already committed, the night is
> flagged and tells you why. It's a warning, not a block: if you know the
> engine can cover both, tick Schedule anyway.

### 5. Taking a seat — 1:00

**On screen:** the night's event page as a member; the Apparatus & Crew card;
Sign me up pressed on an open Elf seat.

**Captions:** Members open the night and see every seat on the apparatus ·
Sign me up takes the seat and marks them as going

**Narration:**

> Members open the night and see every seat. Sign me up takes one and marks
> them as going. A Driver seat is only offered to members who meet the
> apparatus's EVOC level, or have an approved driver exception.

### 6. The coordinator's view — 1:08

**On screen:** the same card as a coordinator, with Change… and Clear on each
seat and Edit this night in the header.

**Captions:** Coordinators can Assign or Change any seat… · Edit this night
changes this night only…

**Narration:**

> Coordinators can assign or change any seat, and for the Driver seat the
> list starts with members who qualify. Edit this night adds or removes seats
> or changes the rules for that one night, without touching the template or
> the other nights.

### 7. On the schedule, not a shift — 1:19

**On screen:** Shift Scheduling, week of December 6; the Monday opened, with
the Outreach card beside the day's shift.

**Captions:** On Shift Scheduling, nights show as Outreach · not a shift ·
Officers see the engine is committed…

**Narration:**

> On Shift Scheduling, apparatus nights appear beside the day's shifts,
> labelled Outreach — not a shift. Officers can see the engine is committed
> that evening, but it never counts toward shift staffing.

### 8. A public request with an apparatus — 1:31

**On screen:** the Outreach Requests tab; Scout Troop 12's scheduled request; the
Apparatus Crew section; _Santamobile — Full_ chosen; a conflict acknowledged;
Add apparatus crew; the crew summary that replaces the picker.

**Captions:** A public request can bring an apparatus too · On a scheduled
request, choose a template under Apparatus Crew · The crew goes on the
request's own calendar event… · Volunteer signups for the same request stay
available

**Narration:**

> When a school or a scout troop asks for an engine, open their request once
> it's scheduled and choose a template under Apparatus Crew. The crew goes on
> the request's own calendar event, so if the visit moves to a new date the
> crew moves with it, and if it's cancelled the crew stands down. You can still
> open volunteer signups for the same visit.
>
> One thing to know: if a request is postponed with no new date, its calendar
> event is cancelled. When you reschedule it, add the crew again.

### 9. Confirm the crew and post hours — 1:49

**On screen:** Apparatus Events, Needs confirmation selected, an ended night
listed with its check-in count.

**Captions:** After the night, it waits under Needs confirmation · Open it and
Finalize Attendance…

**Narration:**

> After the night, it waits under Needs confirmation. Open it, correct any
> times, add anyone who pitched in without signing up, and press Finalize
> Attendance. The crew's hours post to the template's Admin Hours category —
> in place of the event type's usual category, so nobody is credited twice.
> That's apparatus events. The full steps are in the Events guide.

### Not in the 2026-11-17 recording: emailing members about open seats

**Email members** on the crew card was added on 2026-11-20, after the silent
recording was made. To cover it, record one more scene on a coordinator's
crew card: press **Email members**, tick a class or a position, show the
member count, add a note and press **Send email**.

> To fill the run faster, press Email members. Choose who to ask — by member
> class, membership status or position — and the dialog tells you how many
> members that is before you send. One email lists every night that still
> has an open seat. Members who turned off volunteer call emails are skipped.

### Not in the 2026-11-17 recording: approving sign-ups

**Coordinator approves self sign-ups** was added on 2026-11-23. To cover it,
record one more scene: tick the setting in the template editor, then show a
night as a member pressing **Ask for this seat**, and the same night as a
coordinator with the request under the seat and **Approve** pressed.

> If you'd rather choose the crew yourself, tick Coordinator approves self
> sign-ups. Members then ask for a seat instead of taking it, and you're
> emailed. Several can ask for the same seat — approve one, and the others
> are told it went to someone else. Anything you haven't answered by the
> start of the night is declined automatically.

### Scene 8 shows the old request panel

Since 2026-11-21 a request opens on a page of its own rather than in a panel
over the Outreach Requests list, and **Apparatus Crew** sits below **Volunteer
Staffing** on that page. The 2026-11-17 recording of scene 8 shows the old
panel. Its captions and narration still hold, but re-record the scene on the
request's page before publishing, so the footage matches what a coordinator
sees.

## Before publishing
