# Information Security Policy — Skeleton

**[DEPARTMENT: name]** operates The Logbook to manage membership, training,
scheduling, and related records, including protected health information.
Leadership commits to protecting the confidentiality, integrity, and
availability of that information.

## Principles

1. **Least privilege.** Access follows role: members see what membership
   requires; officers and administrators receive only the permissions their
   duties need, granted through the platform's role system and reviewed
   [DEPARTMENT: cadence, e.g. annually and at every role change].
2. **Accountability.** Security-relevant actions are recorded in a
   tamper-evident audit log retained seven years and copied off-host.
3. **Data minimization.** Personal data is collected for department
   operations only, retained per the records-retention schedule, exported or
   anonymized on request per the privacy notice, and never sold.
4. **Secure by default.** Encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest for
   sensitive fields, multi-factor authentication
   [DEPARTMENT: required for all members / required for officer+ roles],
   and vendor-supported software versions kept current.
5. **Incident readiness.** Suspected incidents are reported to
   [DEPARTMENT: role/contact] immediately. HIPAA breach notification has
   statutory deadlines (60 days) — see the incident procedure.

## Responsibilities

- [DEPARTMENT: chief/president] — accountable owner; adopts and reviews
  this policy [DEPARTMENT: annually].
- [DEPARTMENT: IT administrator role] — operates the platform, applies
  updates, manages keys per the cryptography policy, runs restore drills.
- Every member — safeguards their credentials, reports suspected
  compromise, and uses department systems for department business.
