States (https://download.geonames.org/export/zip/US.zip).

4.0 License (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/): it may be used as
long as credit is given to GeoNames, for example with a link to
www.geonames.org. The application's footer carries that credit.

The data is provided "as is" without warranty or any representation of
accuracy, timeliness or completeness. Only the ZIP code, place name, state code
and county are kept. Rebuild with backend/scripts/build_us_zip_codes.py.
