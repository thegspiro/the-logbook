# Why The Logbook

Most departments keep their records in a mix of spreadsheets, paper binders,
group texts and a paid service or two. The Logbook replaces that with one
system your department runs and owns.

[See the features](features.md){ .md-button .md-button--primary }
[Install it](../../wiki/Installation.md){ .md-button }

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

- **Free and open source.** The Logbook is released under the
  [MIT License](../../LICENSE). There is no subscription, no per-member fee and
  no feature held back for a paid tier.
- **Your data stays with you.** It runs on a server your department controls,
  from an Unraid box or a Raspberry Pi in the station to a cloud host. Your
  records live in your own database, and each member can download a copy of
  their own data.
- **Built for fire and EMS.** Shifts and platoons, apparatus and seats,
  training hours and certifications, drills and business meetings: the app
  is organized around how a department actually runs.
- **Works on the phones members already carry.** Install it to a phone's home
  screen from the browser, with no app store, and get push notifications for
  what needs attention. [Mobile guide](../../docs/training/10-mobile-pwa.md)
- **Every department sets its own rules.** Turn modules on and off, define
  positions and permissions, and set compliance thresholds and approval steps
  to match your bylaws.
- **Easy to read.** Light and dark themes are built to strict contrast
  standards, with a high-contrast mode for anyone who needs more.

</div>

## Security your members can trust

The Logbook holds personal and health information, so it is built to protect
it:

- Sign-in with two-factor authentication, or through your department's Google
  or Microsoft accounts.
- Permissions by position, so members see their own records and officers see
  what their role needs.
- Sensitive fields encrypted in the database, and an audit log of who did what
  that shows if it has been altered.
- Designed with HIPAA's requirements in mind. Certifying an installation as
  compliant still needs a review of how your department runs it.

## Getting started

1. **Install it** on any computer that runs Docker, with one command or the
   Unraid template. [Installation](../../wiki/Installation.md)
2. **Set up your department** with the onboarding wizard: your
   administrator account, the modules you want, ranks, stations and
   apparatus.
   [Onboarding](../../wiki/Onboarding.md)
3. **Bring in your members** from a CSV file or add them one at a time.
   [Membership Management](../../docs/training/01-membership.md)

Then share the [training guides](../../docs/training/README.md) with your
members. Each one walks through a module screen by screen.
